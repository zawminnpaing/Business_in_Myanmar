Navigating the Market for You

International commodity trading in Myanmar requires deep local knowledge. At AgriLinkBurma, our core strength lies in our trusted relationships across the agricultural sector. We offer global buyers something highly valuable: Direct Access to Myanmar's Premium Hubs.

We act as your dedicated local partner, connecting you seamlessly with established tier-1 wholesale suppliers and processing mills in Yangon and major regional centers.

Our Role in Your Supply Chain:

  • Market Consolidation: We source from multiple verified hub-suppliers to fulfill bulk orders quickly and efficiently.
  • Quality Verification: We oversee sorting processes and integrate third-party inspections (SGS/Bureau Veritas) prior to loading.
  • Export Logistics: Handling the complex export documentation, phytosanitary certs, and customs clearance seamlessly.
